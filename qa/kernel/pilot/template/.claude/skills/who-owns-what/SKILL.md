# Who Owns What

Use when you are blocked or unsure and need to find out which team or person owns an area of the system.

## Steps

1. Look the folder up in the ownership sheet on the wiki.
2. Ask in the team channel when the sheet has no entry.
3. Add the answer to the sheet for the next person.
