# Email Campaign Builder

Use when composing a marketing newsletter or a promotional HTML email for the customer mailing list, including subject line and layout.

## Steps

1. Start from the approved HTML layout.
2. Keep the subject line under 50 characters.
3. Send a proof to the marketing inbox before scheduling.
