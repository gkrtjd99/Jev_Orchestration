# Dataviz Charts

## Guidelines

1. Pick the form from the question (trend, comparison, distribution, composition).
2. Use the categorical palette in order; highlight at most one series.
3. Direct-label lines; no legends when four or fewer series.
4. Export at deck (1600x900) and document (1200x675) sizes.
