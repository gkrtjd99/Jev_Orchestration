# IP and domain warm-up

1. Start with the most engaged recipients; begin in the low thousands per provider per day.
2. Increase by no more than about 50% per day while complaint and deferral rates stay flat.
3. Hold or step back when a provider starts deferring.
4. Keep the old infrastructure carrying the remainder until the ramp completes.
