# MSA redlining

1. Compare each clause to the playbook position: preferred, fallback, walk-away.
2. Liability cap: preferred 12 months of fees; carve-outs for confidentiality and data breach.
3. Insert tracked changes with a short comment giving the reason.
4. Summarise open points for the business owner.
